#### Red Hat Academy Partnership:

In today’s rapidly evolving technology landscape, preparing students for the real-world challenges of the market is essential. As a partner of CBSoft 2026, Red Hat® Academy invites professors, researchers, and coordinators to visit Room B7 at IME-USP to discover how to bring this transformation to your university at no cost!

Red Hat® Academy is a completely free global program created to help higher education institutions keep pace with the industry by providing practical, up-to-date training in Linux, Cloud, and Application Services. Developed directly with the Red Hat engineering team and built around Red Hat Enterprise Linux®, the market-leading enterprise Linux platform, the curriculum ensures skills aligned with the real-world demands of major companies.

What Red Hat® Academy offers your institution:

* 100% Free Partnership: Enrollment, participation, and the online registration process are completely free of charge for eligible universities.

* Cutting-Edge Curriculum: Content focused on strategic technologies (Linux®, Cloud, and Microservices) to boost students’ employability.

* Exclusive Certification Discounts: Reduced prices on official Red Hat exams for instructors and students.

* Faculty Training: Self-paced courses at no cost for training, certification, and continuous professional development for professors.

* Complete Learning Platform: A web portal for managing classes, assigning tasks, and accessing teaching resources available in up to 9 languages.

* Practical and Flexible Labs: Virtual testing environments that can be adapted to the pedagogical needs of each course.

* Bridge to the Job Market: Access to the Red Hat Academy Talent Network, connecting your students directly with opportunities at Red Hat ecosystem customers and partners.
