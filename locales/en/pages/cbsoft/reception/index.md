To kick off the first day with plenty of interaction, networking, and good food, we invite all participants to our Reception and Opening Cocktail of CBSoft 2026. It will be a relaxed and exclusive gathering, specially prepared to celebrate our community coming together again.

## General Information

- **Date:** September 8, 2026 (Tuesday)
- **Time:** 7:00 PM to 10:00 PM (there will be a few buses leaving from USP; more information coming soon)
- **Location:** Chacrinha – Vila Leopoldina
- **Address:** R. Aroaba, 51 – Vila Leopoldina, São Paulo – SP
- **Access:** The space is reserved exclusively for event participants.

## About the Venue

Chacrinha is a welcoming venue made up of three areas: Pracinha, Jardim, and Paraíso Perdido. The space combines open-air and covered areas designed to provide comfort and a smooth experience [https://www.instagram.com/a_chacrinha/]:

- **Capacity:** Up to 300 people in the covered area and 250 people in the open-air area.
- **Facilities:** The venue will be set up and decorated for the event, with a central bar, 4 restrooms, and a dedicated staff team.

## Live Music
